Add to Google Calendar
Your ORBIT calendar URL

Add this feed to Google Calendar:

1
Open Google Calendar on a desktop browser. In the left sidebar, find Other calendars and click the + button beside it.
2
Select From URL from the menu that appears.
3
Paste your ORBIT calendar URL into the field and click Add calendar. Google Calendar will import it immediately.
4
Find the new calendar in your sidebar — it's usually called ORBIT. Click the three-dot menu beside it to set a color that distinguishes it from your other calendars.
5
Done. The feed refreshes automatically. Your partner uses the same URL but with their own settings — send them to this page to build theirs.
